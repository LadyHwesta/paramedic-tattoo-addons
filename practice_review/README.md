# Practitioner Suite - Reviews

Clients review the practitioner who treated them, from **My Reviews** in
their portal, once they've had a completed appointment: 1 to 5 stars, a
title, the review, how their name is shown, and any of their own shared
photos they choose. Staff check every review under **Practice › Reviews**
before it's published to the **Client Reviews** blog, tagged with the
practitioner. Review posts have comments off. A client can withdraw their
review at any time, which takes the post and its photo copies down.
