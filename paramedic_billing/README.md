# Paramedical Tattoo - Billing

- **Services have a price.** Giving a service a price creates the service
  product its invoices use (Billed As).
- **Create Invoice** on a completed appointment makes the patient's invoice
  for that service, with the visit's date on the line, and links it back: the
  appointment shows Paid / Not Paid.
- **Create Invoices** from the appointments list bills several visits at once.
- **To Invoice** filter: completed appointments not billed yet.

Installs itself when Paramedical Tattoo - Appointments and Invoicing are both
installed. Insurance claims are out of scope: invoices go to the patient.
